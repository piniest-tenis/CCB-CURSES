**Tier 2 Bruiser**
_What was once the village granary is now a semi-corporeal column of fused villagers and rotted grain, rooted where it stood, asymmetric and weeping. Faces press up beneath its skin — some still moving their mouths — and the black water it sweats pools in a dark ring around its base. It does not chase; it waits for the Creep to do the chasing._
**Motives & Tactics**: Hold ground, spread the pool, crush what will not come to the water
***
Difficulty: 14
Thresholds: 14/28
HP: 7
Stress: 4
Attack: +0
Weapon: Crushing Mass: Very Close | 2d12+3 Physical
***
**Features**
**Creep Pool - Passive**: At the start of each GM turn, the area within Close range of the Colossus becomes saturated with pooled Creep (this is exposed Creep). A PC who ends their turn within Close range must succeed on an Agility Reaction Roll (12) or mark a Stress; a character who marks two Stress this way gains the _Creep-Touched_ condition. The pool can be ignited by the Ember-Driven Villager or any fire.
**Semi-Corporeal - Passive**: The Colossus takes half damage from physical attacks.
**Collapse - Action**: Mark a Stress to sag its whole mass. Make an attack against all targets within Very Close range; targets it succeeds against take 2d6+3 physical damage and are knocked back to Close range.
**Overflow - Reaction**: When the Colossus takes damage that marks its Major threshold, the Creep Pool surges. All PCs within Close range must succeed on an Agility Reaction Roll (13) or mark a Stress (two Stress → _Creep-Touched_).
