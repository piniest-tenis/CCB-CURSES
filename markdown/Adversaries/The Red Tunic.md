**Tier 2 Bruiser**
_The child in the red tunic and belt was never resisting — she never needed to drink. She is the forest's own, the one already claimed before the wells were touched, and the village mistook her stillness for innocence. Beneath the tunic she is dihedrally symmetric, too many joints, a face that is only a child's when someone is looking._
**Motives & Tactics**: Lure, isolate, feed on grief, unmake the rescuers
***
Difficulty: 15
Thresholds: 14/28
HP: 7
Stress: 3
Attack: +2
Weapon: Too Many Hands: Melee | 2d12+4 Physical
***
**Features**
**Still in Her - Passive**: She wears the image of the village's last innocent: the child who never drank. Until a PC succeeds on an Instinct Reaction Roll (15) to see what she truly is, all PCs have disadvantage on attack rolls against her — they cannot bring themselves to strike a child. Once pierced, the illusion breaks for the whole party, and what remains is worse.
**Under the Skin - Action**: She presses a too-cold hand to a target within Melee range and leaves a Creep filament beneath the skin. The target must succeed on an Agility Reaction Roll (14) or the filament takes root: at the start of each of the target's turns they mark a Stress and take 1d4 magical damage as it grows toward the heart (two Stress this way → _Creep-Touched_). The filament can be cut out with a Finesse Roll (15), or burned out (the target takes 1d6 fire damage and the effect ends).
**Too Many Joints - Action**: Mark a Stress to move up to Far range in a burst of wrong, jointed motion — a crawl that is too fast — then make a standard attack.
**Noxious Kiss - Reaction**: When a PC within Melee range damages her, she exhales a bloom of Creep into their mouth and eyes. That PC must succeed on an Agility Reaction Roll (14) or take 1d8 magical damage and mark a Stress (two Stress → _Creep-Touched_).
