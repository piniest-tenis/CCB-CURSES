**Tier 2 Bruiser**
_A final-stage reshaped Ayonik the size of a hay-cart, hunched and patient as a reaper. Six arms of fused grain-bone and villager limbs fan from its shoulders; the black crust rings its eyes and mouth, and it moves with the terrible slowness of a body that has already decided what it is. It harvests, and it is not angry — only hungry._
**Motives & Tactics**: Reap, gather, drag the still-living toward the wells
***
Difficulty: 14
Thresholds: 14/28
HP: 7
Stress: 3
Attack: +1
Weapon: Grasping Arms: Very Close | 2d12+2 Physical
***
**Features**
**Noxious Hide - Passive**: The Winnower's skin weeps Creep. All targets within Melee range must succeed on an Agility Reaction Roll (12) or mark a Stress. A character who marks two Stress this way gains the _Creep-Touched_ condition.
**Reap and Gather - Action**: Mark a Stress to make a standard attack against up to two targets within Very Close range. On a success against a target, that target is also dragged into Melee range and Restrained (break free with a Strength Roll (14)).
**Momentum - Reaction**: When the Winnower makes a successful attack against a PC, you gain a Fear.
