**Tier 3 Social**
_Two tons of reshaped flesh, yet she kept her face, her memory, and her voice — because she chose the gift, and the forest lets the willing keep who they were. Mooz is not lying: she genuinely believes the forest is saving this town, and she will press that mercy on you with the tenderness of something that has forgotten you might say no._
**Motives & Tactics**: Compel acceptance, offer the water, know too much, be kind
***
Difficulty: 17
Thresholds: 16/32
HP: 6
Stress: 5
Attack: +0
Weapon: Reshaping Touch: Very Close | 3d4+2 Physical
***
**Features**
**Unbidden Knowing - Passive**: Mooz knows things she was never told. The first time each scene a PC tries to lie to her, conceal a plan, or discuss her in secret, she finishes their sentence or names a memory they never shared. That PC must succeed on a Presence Reaction Roll (17) or mark a Stress and gain disadvantage on their next action roll against her.
**Sincere as the Rot - Passive**: Mooz is not deceiving anyone. Attempts to detect deception, catch her in a lie, or shake her conviction by exposing a falsehood fail automatically — there is no falsehood to expose. A PC may still negotiate, refuse, or persuade her to wait, but they cannot win by proving her wrong.
**Offer the Cup - Action**: Mooz proffers the well-water to a target within Close range. The target must succeed on a Presence Reaction Roll (17) or take one step toward the water and mark a Stress. A target may spend 1 Hope before rolling to gain advantage. If the target chooses to drink, resolve with the Aura of Compulsion's "The Cure Is the Corruption."
**Speak in Their Voice - Action**: Mooz quotes a lost memory or a dead loved one in the character's own remembered voice. The target must succeed on a Presence Reaction Roll (17); on a failure they must spend 1 Hope or mark 2 Stress.
**A Gift Is a Debt - Reaction**: Once per scene, when a PC who has accepted any gift from Mooz or the forest (including drinking the water) takes an action against her, you may spend a Fear to force that PC to mark a Stress or spend a Hope as the forest asserts its claim.
