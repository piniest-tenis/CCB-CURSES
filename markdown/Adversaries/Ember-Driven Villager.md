**Tier 1 Minion**
_A medial-stage villager clutching a stolen ember in a cracked clay cup, eyes hollow as lantern-sockets, jaw slack with lockjaw. They are drawn, inexorably and silently, toward any black water they can see — and they mean to set it burning._
**Motives & Tactics**: Find exposed Creep, light it, burn bright
***
Difficulty: 10
Thresholds: None
HP: 1
Stress: 1
Attack: -2
Weapon: Ember: Melee | 2 Physical
***
**Features**
**Minion (3) - Passive**: This adversary is defeated when they take any damage. For every 3 damage a PC deals to them, defeat an additional Minion within range the attack would succeed against.
**Compelled to Kindle - Passive**: If any exposed Creep is present in the scene, this villager is compelled to ignite it. At the GM's next opportunity it moves to the exposed Creep and lights it, applying the [[Burning Creep]] environment's effects to the scene. It cannot be dissuaded except by being slain, restrained, or the party removing or covering all exposed Creep. It will not attack while there is Creep to burn.
**Group Attack - Action**: Spend a Fear to choose a target and spotlight all Ember-Driven Villagers within Close range of them. Those Minions move into Melee range of the target and make one shared attack roll. On a success, they deal 2 physical damage each. Combine this damage.
