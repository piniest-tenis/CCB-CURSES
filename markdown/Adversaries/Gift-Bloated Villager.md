**Tier 1 Minion**
_A medial-stage villager whose belly is grossly distended with Creep-touched well-water, lips black-crusted, still muttering "thank the forest" as they shamble. They beg you to drink even as they leak. Killing them is the mistake._
**Motives & Tactics**: Entreat, embrace, burst
***
Difficulty: 10
Thresholds: None
HP: 1
Stress: 1
Attack: -2
Weapon: Grasping Hands: Melee | 2 Physical
***
**Features**
**Minion (3) - Passive**: This adversary is defeated when they take any damage. For every 3 damage a PC deals to them, defeat an additional Minion within range the attack would succeed against.
**Creep Payload - Passive**: When this adversary is defeated, they burst. All targets within Close range must succeed on an Agility Reaction Roll (12) or mark a Stress (two Stress → _Creep-Touched_). Their body then leaves exposed Creep in its space, which an Ember-Driven Villager can ignite.
**Group Attack - Action**: Spend a Fear to choose a target and spotlight all Gift-Bloated Villagers within Close range of them. Those Minions move into Melee range of the target and make one shared attack roll. On a success, they deal 2 physical damage each. Combine this damage.
