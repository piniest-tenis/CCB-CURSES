*The town center, where the wells run black and the gift of the forest presses on every mind like a hand. The water is there, cool and clean-looking, and Mooz's voice is warm — and every moment that passes, holding your ground costs you a little more of yourself.*
**Tone**: Oppressive, cloying, uncanny, patient, dread
**Tier 2**
**Type**: Social
**Difficulty**: 13

**Potential Adversaries**:
- [[The Winnower]]
- [[The Granary Colossus]]
- [[The Red Tunic]]
- [[Gift-Bloated Villager]]
- [[Ember-Driven Villager]]
- [[Mooz, Keeper of the Gift]]

**Features**
**The Pull of the Gift - Passive**
At the end of each GM turn, each PC in the town center must choose: spend 1 Hope to hold their ground, or mark 1 Stress and take one step toward the wells (gaining the _Drawn_ condition). A PC who reaches the wells must either drink or succeed on a Presence Reaction Roll (13) to turn away.
_"You know you could just drink. It would be so much easier than standing there fighting it."_

**The Cure Is the Corruption - Passive**
A PC who drinks the well-water immediately clears all active curses and conditions and clears all marked Stress — the temptation is real and immediate. But drinking also means accepting the gift: the PC advances one corruption stage (Initial → Medial → Final), gains a persistent Creep-taint, and becomes tied to Mooz and the forest.
_"The water takes the curse. It takes everything else, too — and gives it back wearing a new face."_

**Creep-Slick Wells - Passive**
The wells and their water are exposed Creep. A PC who touches the water, enters Melee range of a well, or stands in pooled Creep must succeed on an Agility Reaction Roll (12) or mark a Stress (two Stress → _Creep-Touched_). This exposed Creep can be ignited by the Ember-Driven Villager.
_"The black water is the gift, and the gift is eager to be touched."_

**Press the Cup - Active**
Spend a Fear. A medial villager (or Mooz) presses water on a _Drawn_ PC. That PC must succeed on a Presence Reaction Roll (13) or drink (resolve "The Cure Is the Corruption"). A PC may spend 1 Hope before rolling to gain advantage.
_"A hand, soft and insistent, tilts the cup toward your lips. 'It's time for a drink,' she says."_

**The Creep Takes Light - Reaction**
When any exposed Creep in the scene is ignited (by an Ember-Driven Villager, or any fire), the scene gains the [[Burning Creep]] environment's effects. Each PC must immediately succeed on an Agility Reaction Roll (13) or take 1d6 magical damage from the noxious smoke (not reduced by Armor).
_"The moment the ember touches the water, the black surface catches — and the town begins to burn sweet and slow."_
